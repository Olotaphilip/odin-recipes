Tombrown cereal recipe

How to prepare tombrown

Tombrown in a very nutritious cereal high in protein, that's also very easy to prepare, i'll show you step by step how too

ingredients needed

  • tombrown powder
  • water
  • milk
  • sugar

Steps

  1. put two cups of water in a kettle to boil
  2. mix 5 spoon of the tombrown powder with very little warm water
  3. pour about half cup of the boiling water into the tombrown powder and keep mixing till it teak
  4. add your sugar and milk till taste
  5. enjoy!
