Jollof rice recipe

How to prepare nigeria jollof rice

Jollof rice is a very tasty meal common in Nigeria, it's a meal no one can recist.

in this recipe i'll show you how to wow other with a plate of jollof rice even as a programmer who no one will expect can cook well

lets get into it!

Here are the list of ingredients you'll be needing

  • five cups of rice
  • one kilo of meat
  • five cubes of maggi
  • 1 spoon of salt
  • halfcup of groundnut oil
  • sachet curry and thyme
  • fresh tomatoes
  • pepper the small one and the big one
  • onions
  • one sachet tomatoes

Steps

  1. boil your meat with two maggi cude, add a teaspoon of salt add little curry and thyme, then let it cook for 20 minutes
  2. when the meat is ready, remove it from the water and fry it, then keep is aside
  3. blend your tomatoes together with your onions and pepper, then boil it till it very thick(when the all the water have evaporated).
  4. now get a clean pot, put the groundnut oil inside that you used in frying your meat. use that oil to fry your boiled tomatoes, add your curry, thyme, 4 maggi cudes and the ramaining salt
  5. add your sachet tomatoes
  6. after frying for about 15 minutes, add your meat water(the water you used in boiling your meat) into the tomatoes, cover and let it boil for another 15 minutes
  7. wash your rice 5 time(make sure you'er using stoneless rice) and add it to your boiling tomatoes, make sure the rice is slightly below the tomatoes, then cook under low heat for 30 minutes to 40 minutes
  8. serve yourself and your guest!
